Privacy Policy

Kandy Premium Cleaning ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website (kandypremiumcleaning.lk) and use our professional cleaning services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when booking a service, including:

  • Name
  • Contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Service address (where the cleaning will take place)
  • Payment details (processed securely via PayHere; we do not store your full card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Process and schedule your cleaning bookings.
  • Process payments securely through our third-party payment gateway (PayHere).
  • Communicate with you regarding service updates, inquiries, and support.

3. Security of Your Information

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data. All online payments are handled securely by PayHere, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We do not store or have access to your raw payment card credentials.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at info@kandypremiumcleaning.lk.
