Privacy Policy

At Kandy Premium Cleaning, your privacy is highly critical to us. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safe-keep your transactional and personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide when booking a service, including your name, telephone number, residential/business address, and email address.

2. Payment & Data Security

All online payment transactions are processed securely through PayHere, a central authorized payment gateway in Sri Lanka. We do not store your credit card numbers, CVV code, or banking credentials on our web servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and fulfill your requested cleaning services.
  • To send transactional receipts, booking confirmations, or service updates.
  • To continuously improve our customer care and service operations.

4. Third-Party Disclosure

We strictly do not sell, trade, or share your personally identifiable information with third parties, except as required legally by Sri Lankan authorities or partner banks to verify transactions.
