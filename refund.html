Refund & Cancellation Policy

Thank you for choosing Kandy Premium Cleaning. We aim to ensure maximum customer satisfaction. Please read our refund and booking cancellation policy details below.

1. Cancellation Policy

  • Customers can cancel or reschedule a cleaning appointment up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled service time without any penalty.
  • Cancellations made within less than 24 hours of the appointment may incur a small handling fee.

2. Refund Conditions

If a customer is genuinely dissatisfied with the quality of our service, they must report it within 24 hours of completion. Our team will inspect and re-clean the disputed area free of charge. If a resolution cannot be met, a partial or full refund will be processed under management evaluation.

3. Mandatory Payment Method Policy

Please note that the refund will be given to the payment-initiated media itself on your website.

This means if you paid using a specific credit card, debit card, or online bank account via PayHere, the approved refund will be directly credited back to that exact payment method. Refunds cannot be dispersed via alternative methods (such as cash or third-party accounts).

4. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are typically finalized and credited back within 3 to 7 working days, subject to standard processing times mandated by partner banks and PayHere.
